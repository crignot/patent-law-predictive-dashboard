#### What the largest errors show

- **Confident "granted" predictions that were abandoned** all had a first observed action that already indicated allowable claims, mostly in networking, semiconductors, and software (TC 2400 / 2800 / 2100). Five of the top eight were abandoned for *"Failure to Respond to an Office Action"* and three for *"Failure to Pay Issue Fee"*, meaning the application was effectively allowed and then dropped. The examination record looked favorable; the applicant walked away. The model has no signal for applicant resources or intent (cost, priorities, a changed business plan), which is a real limit of predicting outcomes from examination data alone.
- **Low "granted" scores that were granted** fall into two groups: chemical applications (TC 1700) with unusually long §103 rejections (12–18 references combined), and business-method applications (TC 3600) whose first observed action raised §101, §102, §103, and §112 rejections together. Most such applications are abandoned, but these were pursued and eventually allowed. Persistence after a heavy first action is not visible at the prediction moment.
- **By technology center,** ranking quality is lowest in chemical and biotech (ROC-AUC about 0.64) and highest in business methods (about 0.72). Predicted and actual grant rates stay within about 3 points in every center with meaningful volume.
- **Calibration** is close to the diagonal on the 2015–2017 test years; the lowest-probability decile is slightly under-predicted (about 45% predicted vs. 47% observed).
